How LabLens Works

A safe, 3-step pipeline designed for clarity and peace of mind.

1

Enter your values

Input your numbers from your routine blood report. Choose your biological sex so ranges are accurate. No sign-up or personal identifying information is ever requested.

2

Deterministic range check

Our rule-based engine compares each value against peer-reviewed adult reference ranges from MedlinePlus and Mayo Clinic Laboratories. No AI is used for clinical classification.

3

Plain-language insights

For any flagged values, guarded AI explains general biological roles at an 8th-grade reading level, along with structured questions to bring to your next healthcare appointment.

🛡️

What LabLens is NOT

  • Not a medical diagnostic tool: LabLens cannot and does not diagnose illnesses or conditions.
  • Not medical advice or treatment: It never recommends medications, dosages, or self-treatment therapies.
  • Not a substitute for your doctor: Clinical decisions must always be made with a licensed physician who knows your complete medical history.
🔒

Strict Privacy Guarantee

Your health data stays yours. LabLens processes lab values completely in-memory. We have no database, we do not store health entries, and our application logs record only anonymous request identifiers and status codes—never patient inputs.
