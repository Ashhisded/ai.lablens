About LabLens & Clinical Methodology

LabLens was built to bridge the health literacy gap by helping patients understand their routine blood test reports before and after speaking with their healthcare provider.

1. Deterministic Rules Engine

Clinical safety begins with total determinism. LabLens never uses artificial intelligence to classify blood values. Classification is handled by a pure, rule-based Python engine that compares your exact numeric inputs against reference interval thresholds loaded from a validated clinical schema.

Classification Tiers

  • CRITICAL LOW: Below the critical emergency threshold (immediate medical consultation needed).
  • LOW: Below the lower reference limit for healthy adults.
  • NORMAL: Within the typical reference interval (inclusive of boundary values).
  • HIGH: Above the upper reference limit for healthy adults.
  • CRITICAL HIGH: Above the critical emergency threshold (immediate medical consultation needed).

2. Reference Range Sources & Review

Our reference ranges are curated from trusted clinical public references:

  • MedlinePlus: National Library of Medicine (NIH), medlineplus.gov
  • Mayo Clinic Laboratories: Clinical Reference Guide, mayocliniclabs.com

Last Clinical Review: January 1, 2024 • Version: 1.0.0

3. Safe AI Explanations & Guardrails

Artificial intelligence (Google Gemini Flash) is used exclusively as an educational translation layer for tests that fall outside the normal range:

Strict System Prompting

Enforces an 8th-grade reading level, requires hedging language ("can be associated with"), and forbids diagnosing conditions.

Negative Blocklist Filtering

A post-generation regex filter scans all output for prohibited phrases ("you have", "diagnosed with", medication names, dosages).

Instant Static Fallback

If the AI is unreachable, times out (≤ 8s budget), or generates flagged text, built-in clinical explanations are displayed seamlessly.

4. Known Limitations

Please keep the following constraints in mind when using LabLens:

  • Adult General Population: Ranges apply to non-pregnant adults aged 18 and older. Pediatric, geriatric, and obstetric ranges vary widely.
  • Inter-laboratory Variance: Different commercial laboratories (e.g. Quest, Labcorp) use different analytical assays and instruments, leading to slightly different normal ranges. Always prioritize the reference interval printed on your actual report.
  • Isolated Data Points: A single abnormal blood test value rarely tells the whole story; clinical diagnosis requires physical exam, clinical history, and symptom correlation.

5. Privacy Architecture

LabLens operates under a zero-persistence model. Submitted lab values exist only in memory during the duration of the HTTP request and are discarded immediately. No health information is written to disk, stored in databases, or included in application log files.
