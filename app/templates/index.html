Understand Your Lab Results

Enter values from your blood test report below. LabLens will compare them to typical adult reference ranges and give you plain-language explanations of anything outside the norm.

How to use this tool

  1. Select your biological sex — some ranges differ between males and females.
  2. Enter the numeric values from your lab report in the matching fields. Leave fields blank if a test wasn't done.
  3. Click Analyse Results.
  4. Review the colour-coded table and plain-language explanations.
  5. Always discuss any abnormal results with your doctor.
Where do I find these values?

Your lab values are on the printed or digital report from your doctor's office, hospital, or testing laboratory. Look for a column labelled "Result" or "Value" next to each test name. The units (e.g., g/dL, mg/dL) are shown alongside. If you are unsure which number to enter, ask your healthcare provider.
